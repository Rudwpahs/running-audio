# PR1 Canonical Positioning

Date: 2026-08-16

This document overrides older wording that described PR1 as outdoor group audio, youth camp audio, generic wireless audio, or an earbud-like device.

## The rule

PR1 must always be explained from the **phone ↔ receiver exchange** structure.

Wrong starting point:

> A wireless audio device for studying or parks.

Correct starting point:

> A system where the user leaves their phone at the transmitter/storage station and receives a small receiver that plays audio only.

## Korean one-liner

> PR1은 스터디카페와 공원에서 휴대폰을 맡기고 수신기를 받아 소리만 듣는 시스템입니다.

## Short English one-liner

> PR1 lets people hand in their phone and carry only audio.

## Core flow

```text
1. User arrives at a study cafe or park station.
2. User hands in their phone.
3. The phone is connected to or placed in a PR1 transmitter/storage station.
4. User receives a matched PR1 receiver.
5. User hears audio only: music, lecture, timer, workout audio, or white noise.
6. User cannot casually check the phone screen during use.
7. User returns the receiver.
8. The matched phone is returned.
```

## Why existing earbuds are different

| Existing earbuds | PR1 |
|---|---|
| Phone stays near the user | Phone is left at the PR1 station |
| Screen is still one touch away | Screen access is intentionally separated |
| User owns and carries the setup | Space/operator can run the exchange system |
| Solves wireless listening | Solves phone proximity while keeping audio |
| Competes on sound/comfort | Competes on behavior and operating flow |

PR1 can use earbuds, wired earphones, open-ear output, or bone conduction as the final audio output. Those are output choices, not the product definition.

## Beachhead 1 — Study cafe / 독서실

### Problem

A user may need audio for:

- music
- white noise
- lectures
- timers
- short alerts

But the phone screen is the distraction. Existing earbuds do not remove the phone from the desk or pocket.

### PR1 answer

The phone is deposited at the station. The user keeps only a receiver at the seat. Audio remains available, but casual screen checking becomes difficult.

### First validation question

> Will study cafe users actually hand in their phone if they can still hear the audio they need?

## Beachhead 2 — Park / walking / running

### Problem

A user wants sound while walking, running, or resting in a park, but does not want to carry or check the phone.

### PR1 answer

The phone stays at a PR1 station or controlled point. The user moves with a receiver only.

### First validation question

> Will park users accept a phone deposit / receiver rental flow for walking or running audio?

## Customer types

### Users

- study cafe users
- 독서실 users
- students and exam takers
- park walkers
- runners
- people who want audio without phone access

### Operators / buyers

- study cafe owners
- 독서실 owners
- park program operators
- public/private park service operators
- running/walking program operators

Do not over-focus on general schools, youth camps, or tourism unless evidence specifically points there later.

## Words to avoid

Avoid:

- wireless earbuds
- smart audio device
- screen-free group audio
- outdoor education audio
- digital wellness platform
- youth concentration solution
- open-ear group audio system
- futuristic audio network

These make PR1 look vague or like existing products.

## Words to use

Use:

- phone deposit
- receiver handoff
- phone ↔ receiver exchange
- audio-only receiver
- study cafe station
- park station
- phone in, receiver out
- phone stays away, sound stays with you

## Product test gate

Before any large design or manufacturing work, PR1 must prove:

1. Users will hand in the phone.
2. Audio-only still covers the use case.
3. The phone/receiver matching process feels safe.
4. Operators can distribute, charge, clean, and recover receivers without too much friction.
5. The station can be operated in study cafe and park scenarios.

## Final reminder

PR1 is not defined by the receiver.

PR1 is defined by the exchange:

> **phone in → receiver out → audio only → receiver back → phone back**
